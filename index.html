How this page gets its numbers

  1. Balances — Blockscout /addresses/{safe} and /token-balances on Robinhood Chain.
  2. Price — GeckoTerminal spot price for SHYT on the robinhood network.
  3. Custody — eth_call to the Safe for getOwners(), getThreshold() and VERSION().
  4. History — Blockscout ERC-20 transfer log for the Safe address.

Everything runs in your browser against public endpoints. There is no server, no database, and no account. Treasury value moves with SHYT's market price, so it can fall as well as rise even when no tokens leave the Safe.
